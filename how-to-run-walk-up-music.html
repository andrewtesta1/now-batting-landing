How to Run Walk-Up Music for Youth Baseball & Softball

Now Batting is built to make running walk-up music easy enough that you probably won’t need a manual. But every Chief Hype Officer eventually has a “wait, how do I do that?” moment.

So this is the field guide for the parent with the speaker: quick how-tos for collecting walk-up songs, setting up your roster, getting the batting order ready, running music without cell service, handing things off to another parent, and everything else that somehow becomes your responsibility on game day.

We’ll keep adding to it as new questions, features, and Speaker Parent problems come up.

How to Set Up a Walk-Up Song Roster

Names. Numbers. Walk-up songs. You can type them all in yourself — or send one link and let the parents do it for you.
