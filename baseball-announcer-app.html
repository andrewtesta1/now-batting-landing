Stop Paying for Every Player Announcement

Make an intro for every player on your roster, in any of six voices, and change them as often as you like. It’s all part of one subscription.

The nickel-and-diming gets old

You’re not making one announcement. You’re making twelve, in March, before you’ve heard a single one of them out loud.

Then a player joins in May. Somebody changes their number. A name comes out wrong and you want to redo it. You hear the Hype voice and wonder how the whole lineup would sound in it. A kid who was fine with “now batting” in April decides he’s a “stepping up” guy by July.

Every one of those is a normal Tuesday for the parent running the speaker. None of them should come with a price tag, a credit balance, or a moment where you decide it isn’t worth it and the kid gets the wrong name all season.

With Now Batting, make as many as you want, as many times as you want. Redo the whole roster in a different voice on the drive to the field if you feel like it. It costs the same as making none.

It should sound real

Now Batting uses current, high-quality voice models, so an announcement sounds like somebody saying your player’s name — not a flat, clipped, old-school robot reading it out.

And a name that doesn’t sound the way it looks is the whole game. Name Lock lets you hear a name before anyone else does and fix it once — type how it sounds, or pick from suggestions. From then on that exact pronunciation plays in the walk-up, in the lineup introduction and from the bullpen. You lock it once, not once per game.

Six voices, six different vibes

Tap any of them to hear the same line. Switch the whole roster to a different voice whenever you want — it re-records in the background, and your own uploaded recordings are never overwritten.
