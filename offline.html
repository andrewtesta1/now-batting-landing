Quick answers

Do I have to download every song one by one?

No. Now Batting builds the one playlist for you — you just tap Download on it in Apple Music.

What if I add a new walk-up song later?

Tap Save again to rebuild the playlist, then download it once more. Quick, and worth doing before game day.

Does Roll Call work offline?

Yes — build it on Wi-Fi first. Once generated, the full Roll Call is saved to your phone and plays with no signal. Creating a new one still needs a connection, so build it before you go.

What about Situational Sounds?

They're Apple Music tracks, so make sure you finish the Save + Download step before game day. After that, they're ready to play with no signal.

More offline questions

Do original Now Batting songs need downloading?

No. Your original songs are saved to your phone the moment they're made — they play offline with nothing extra to do.

Do announcer calls work offline?

Yes. They're stored on your phone too, so every "Now batting…" plays with no signal.

Do the curated Music Hub playlists work offline?

When you tap Save, Now Batting builds one playlist with your players' walk-up songs and folds in the curated Now Batting list too — that's what downloads for the field. The other themed playlists in the Music Hub are for browsing on Wi-Fi; they aren't downloaded on their own. Want songs from one at the field? Assign them to your players — then Save covers them like the rest of your lineup.

What if I switch the announcer voice?

Switching re-records your team in the new voice, which needs a connection — so do it on Wi-Fi before you go. Once it's done, the new calls are saved to your phone and play offline. Switching back to a voice you've used before works even with no signal.

Someone else is running the game — will it work offline for them?

Yes. Have them import the roster on Wi-Fi, then tap Save and download the Apple Music playlist on their own phone (they'll need their own Apple Music). After that, they're field-ready too.
