Common questions

No sound during the game. What do I do?

Check that your iPhone's silent switch (the physical toggle on the side) is off. The app plays audio in silent mode, but make sure your volume is turned up before you leave home. If it's still silent, force-quit the app and reopen it.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

You need Apple Music to search the catalog and use those songs as walk-up music. The built-in soundboard (air horn, charge, etc.) and AI player announcements both work without a subscription.

The announcer is saying a name wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes, and you only have to do it once. Open the player and use Name Lock — you'll hear how the name will be said, and if it's wrong you can pick from a few takes or spell it out the way it sounds. Once you lock it, that exact pronunciation is used everywhere: the walk-up, the Roll Call, and the Bullpen. If you later change the player's name or the announcer voice, Name Lock asks you to give it one more listen.

I can't make Saturday's game. How do I hand off the music?

Open the roster menu and tap Share. Send the link to whoever is running the music. They tap it, import a copy of the roster, and they're ready — every player, every song, every announcement already built. Their copy is theirs to run game day with; you stay in control of the original.

If you update the roster on your end after sharing, share again with the same link. They tap Get latest version to pull your changes down to their copy. Edits they make stay on their phone — they're running game day from their copy, you're running it from yours.

Can parents add their own players?

Yes. Share a roster-building link to your team group chat — each parent fills in their own player's name, number, walk-up song, and drop point. It all lands in your roster, ready for you to review and fine-tune.

The field has no cell service. Will the app still work?

Yes, if you set it up before you leave. Everything Now Batting makes for you — announcer calls, original songs, sound effects — is saved to your phone the moment it's made. Apple Music tracks are the one part that needs a step, because an Apple Music song only plays without signal once Apple Music has downloaded it. So while you still have wifi, tap Save on the Manage screen: it gathers your walk-ups, the Situational Sounds and the Now Batting playlist into one place and pulls it all down at once. After that the whole game runs in airplane mode.

How do I change the announcer voice?

Tap the roster name at the top of the main screen, then open Settings. You'll see the two voices — Classic and Energetic. The new voice applies to everything you generate from then on. Announcements you'd already made keep the old voice until you regenerate them, so if you want the whole lineup to match, re-generate each player. Your other teams aren't affected. Roll Call will show Rebuild, and any locked names will ask for a quick re-listen in the new voice.

I found a bug.

Email cho@nowbattingapp.com with what happened. Include your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About). We take bugs seriously and usually ship a fix within a few days.
