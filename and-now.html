And Now… you know the part.

If you’ve ever heard a big-time basketball starting lineup, you know the moment. The music builds. The announcer takes a breath. “Aaaaand now…” And suddenly, five players are the most important people in the building.

We figured the players running out at 9 AM on a Saturday deserved a little of that, too. So we built And Now, the basketball side of Now Batting. Same big-game feeling. Slightly smaller arena. Significantly more parents holding Stanley cups.

Add your roster, pick an announcer and hit Play. No microphone. No memorizing names. No awkward silence while you figure out who wears number 12.

One tap. The whole starting lineup.

Nobody wants to DJ, announce and chase a last-minute lineup change at the same time. And Now runs the entrance start to finish, in the order a big game would.

  1. The welcome. Set the scene. Add your gym’s name if you want it said, and pick today, tonight, this afternoon or this morning. Because “tonight” at 8:30 AM is a tough start.
  2. The visiting team.Optional The full arena treatment or a shorter, standard introduction. Add their starters, bench and coaches if you have them.
  3. The officials.Optional A proper introduction for the people about to hear “THAT’S A FOUL!” for the rest of the game.
  4. Your bench and coaches.Optional But let’s be honest. Coach has been waiting for this moment.
  5. Your Starting 5. Numbers, names and positions, with big announcer energy. Every player gets their moment. Positions can be turned off.
  6. The grand finale. The matchup, then “Let’s play some basketball!” and the music swells.

One Play button runs the whole thing. Leave something out and the script adjusts: no weird gaps, no announcing a coach who didn’t show up. You bring the roster. We’ll bring the goosebumps.

Questions

Is there an app that announces youth basketball starting lineups?

Yep. You’re looking at it. Add your roster, choose your Starting 5, pick an announcer, and And Now builds a complete arena-style introduction with music underneath: the welcome, the visiting team, the officials, the benches, the coaches and the starting lineups. Include what you want, skip what you don’t, then hit Play. It also handles the PA calls once the game starts. It doesn’t keep score. The scorer’s table still does that.

Is And Now a separate basketball app?

Nope. And Now is basketball inside Now Batting. Create a basketball team and the app switches to basketball. Pick your baseball or softball team and you’re back to walk-up songs. One app, multiple sports, one very committed speaker parent.

Can both teams get the full introduction?

Yes. Give the visitors the full arena treatment or a shorter, standard introduction. Your team always gets the full one. Everybody deserves some hype, even the team about to try to beat yours.

Can I announce the benches, coaches and referees?

Yes. They’re all optional parts of the introduction. Include them or skip them.

Can I choose my announcer?

Yep. Five basketball announcer voices, each with its own style. Hear them before you choose, and switch any time.

Does it keep score or track stats?

No. And Now handles the introductions, the PA calls and the music. The scorer’s table keeps the score. We know our lane. It’s usually next to a Bluetooth speaker.

Do I need Wi-Fi in the gym?

No. Your introductions and PA calls are saved to your phone. Use Court Ready ahead of time to bring your Arena jams too.

Can someone else run the announcements?

Yes. Share your team and another person can run the recordings from their iPhone, free for 30 days, without creating an account. They’ll need the latest version of Now Batting.

Does basketball cost extra?

Nope. Basketball is included in your Now Batting subscription, along with baseball and softball.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Now Batting is available for iPhone.
