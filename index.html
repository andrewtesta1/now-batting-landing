You pick the song, drag a marker to the exact moment you want it to kick in, and record or generate a player announcement. On game day: tap the player's card → song starts at your mark → the announcer calls their name → song roars back → fades out. Every beat of that is yours to set, player by player — three sliders in the song editor: where the song drops, when the announcer comes in over it (1.5 seconds in, by default), and where the song fades (a 6.5-second blast after the call, by default). Same three on an Apple Music track or an original we wrote.
Each player gets their own big-league call — "Now batting, number seven, Mikey Moonshot!" You pick how it sounds:

— BYO Announcer: Maybe you have a talent for announcing players — or you already paid for a voice. Upload your own.

Every player's name gets said right — that's the point. If a name is tricky, Name Lock lets you lock how it's said once (pick from a few takes, or spell it your way). From then on the announcer nails it every time — in the walk-up, the Roll Call, and the Bullpen. No more cringing at the plate.
